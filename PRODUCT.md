# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Argentine adults (25–45) who earn pesos, hold some savings in dollars (official or blue), pay with credit cards in installments (cuotas), and want to actually understand where their money goes. They distrust vague app promises, live through inflation every week, and are tired of products built for other economies. They open the app on their phone, often on mobile data, and expect it to feel fast and native despite being a web app.

## Product Purpose

mangui is a personal finance app built specifically for the Argentine economic reality: multiple dollar types (Blue, MEP, CCL, oficial), credit-card resumen cycles, installment tracking (cuotas), and inflation context. It includes Manguito, a built-in AI assistant for querying balances and logging expenses in natural language. The landing page exists to convert curious visitors into registered users or demo viewers — the product itself is the proof.

## Brand Personality

Serio pero cool. Direct, grounded, Argentine. Not playful-startup, not bank-formal. Confident enough to show the product, opinionated enough to name the real problems, warm enough to feel human.

Three words: directo, confiable, nuestro.

Emotional goal: "this was made for me" — the relief of a product that actually understands ARS, cuotas, and inflation without explanation.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS blue-gradient "manage your finances" landing — not our register.
- Testimonials, user-count metrics, press logos — none. Product is the proof.
- WhatsApp-bot "fintech" aesthetics — do not reference bots.
- Bank UI formality — mangui is personal, not institutional.
- Warm-cream/sand "wellness app" palette — wrong emotional register.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product, not a promise.** Every section that talks about a feature shows a coded mockup of it. No abstract illustrations, no vague icons-on-cards as sole evidence.
2. **Argentine reality first.** Copy and mockup data should feel immediately local: pesos, cuotas, Blue/MEP/CCL, inflación. Not "your finances" — "tu plata".
3. **Manguito is a feature, not a gimmick.** The AI assistant earns its section by demonstrating a concrete, realistic conversation — not a chatbot pitch.
4. **Dark anchors, lime/orange pulse.** Premium dark sections ground the page; lime (#84CC16) and orange (#F97316) are accents that carry energy, not decoration.
5. **Demo is the CTA.** "Ver demo" must be as prominent as "Crear cuenta". The demo account is the best sales tool.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as baseline. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. All interactive elements keyboard-navigable with visible focus rings. Reduce-motion respected via existing CSS utilities. Spanish (es-AR) only.
